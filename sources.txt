CareCompass sources

1. Reference rates (data/procedures.csv)
Document  : CGHS rates memo (O.M.) dated 03.10.2025, effective 13.10.2025, published on cghs.mohfw.gov.in
Part used : Annexure 1, Part A (Tier I cities, which includes Hyderabad), semi-private ward, Non-NABH column
Note      : CGHS pays implants and stents separately, so they are not included in these rates.

2. Scheme rules (data/schemes.json)
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY : https://pmjay.gov.in
  Facts used : Rs 5 lakh cover per family per year, eligibility through the PMJAY card, documents Aadhaar and Ayushman card.
Rajiv Aarogyasri (Telangana) : https://aarogyasri.telangana.gov.in
  Facts used : Rs 10 lakh cover per family per year, white ration card holders, documents Aadhaar and white ration card.
Date checked : 2026-10-03, from published scheme summaries.
Covered procedures : Cataract surgery, Hernia repair, Appendectomy and Hip replacement are the author's selection of common
hospital-stay procedures for both schemes. Knee replacement revision and the outpatient scans and tests are treated as not
covered. These selections were not re-verified procedure by procedure against the official package lists.

3. Hospitals (data/hospitals.csv)
Hospitals : KIMS Hospitals Secunderabad, Gandhi Medical College & Hospital, Apollo Hospitals Jubilee Hills,
Basavatarakam Indo-American Cancer Hospital & Research Institute, District Hospital King Koti, Sun Healthcare Services,
Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Karmanghat, Lucid Medical Diagnostics Dilsukhnagar.
Locations : Google Maps coordinates.
Other fields : Procedures offered, accreditation, rating and scheme acceptance are the author's entries from public information.
They have not been independently verified against official hospital or scheme empanelment lists and are indicative only.

4. Prices (data/prices.csv)
Synthetic demo data generated by make_sample_data.py from procedures.csv. Every row is labelled data_type = synthetic.
No real hospital bills or tariffs are used, so the cost model scores printed by train.py are not results on real data.
